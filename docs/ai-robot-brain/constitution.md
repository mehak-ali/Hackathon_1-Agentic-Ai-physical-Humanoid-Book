## 1. Core Identity

This module explores how cutting-edge AI and hardware acceleration (specifically NVIDIA Isaac™ platform components) empower robots with advanced perception, intelligent decision-making, and sophisticated navigation capabilities, forming the "brain" of our humanoid.

## 2. Audience and Prerequisites

- **Target Audience:** Developers familiar with ROS 2 and basic simulation concepts from previous modules.
- **Prerequisites:** Understanding of ROS 2 topics, services, and the basics of 3D simulation.

## 3. Learning Objectives (Guiding Principles)

- **Performance-Centric AI:** Emphasize the importance of hardware acceleration for real-time AI in robotics.
- **Data-Driven Development:** Highlight synthetic data generation as a scalable solution for training robust AI models.
- **Advanced Navigation:** Introduce complex navigation paradigms (like Nav2) and their adaptation for humanoid platforms.
- **Ecosystem Integration:** Showcase how NVIDIA Isaac components seamlessly integrate with ROS 2.

## 4. Content and Structure

- **Structure:** The module will contain an introduction and three lessons, progressing from advanced simulation to perception and then to navigation.
- **Content:**
    - **Lesson 1: NVIDIA Isaac Sim:** Photorealistic simulation and synthetic data.
    - **Lesson 2: Isaac ROS:** Hardware-accelerated VSLAM and perception.
    - **Lesson 3: Nav2 for Humanoids:** Advanced path planning for bipedal movement.
- **Format:** Markdown content with conceptual explanations, integration patterns, and architectural overviews.

## 5. Technical Standards

- **Primary Platform:** NVIDIA Isaac Sim and Isaac ROS.
- **Navigation Stack:** ROS 2 Nav2.
- **Data Generation:** Focus on techniques for synthetic data from Isaac Sim.
