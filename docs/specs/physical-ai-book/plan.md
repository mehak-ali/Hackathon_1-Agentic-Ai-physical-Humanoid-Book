# Plan for "The Embodied Mind: A Guide to Building a Physical Humanoid AI"

## Introduction

This book is a comprehensive guide to designing, building, and programming a physical humanoid robot with artificial intelligence. It is intended for hobbyists, students, and researchers who are interested in the intersection of robotics, AI, and cognitive science. The reader will learn the fundamentals of mechanical design, electronics, and AI, and will be guided through the process of building their own humanoid robot from the ground up.

## Project-Based Learning

The book will follow a project-based approach, where the reader will build a humanoid robot from scratch. Each chapter will include practical exercises and code examples.
