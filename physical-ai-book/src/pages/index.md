# Welcome to "Physical AI: The Humanoid Robotic Book"

This book explores the fascinating world where artificial intelligence meets physical embodiment. Dive into the concepts, technologies, and implications of creating humanoid robots capable of interacting with and learning from our physical world.

## What you will learn:

*   **The Basics:** From bits to atoms, understanding the fundamental components of a physical AI system.
*   **Sensing and Perception:** How robots gather information about their environment.
*   **Actuation and Action:** How robots move and manipulate objects in the real world.
*   **Cognition and Learning:** The AI algorithms that enable humanoids to think, learn, and adapt.
*   **Ethical Considerations:** The societal impact and responsibilities of building advanced physical AI.

We invite you to embark on this journey with us, exploring the present and future of humanoid robotics and physical AI.
